## 5. Recipe-system implications

* CrossTie ≈ ossover recipe: `bottletemplate`→`winver`+arch, `predependency`→`winetricks`/`file` steps, `preinstallenvironment`→`env` (written **into the bottle**, persistent), `installerenvironment`→per-install env, `preinstallregistry`/`postinstallregistry`→`registry`, `installerdlloverrides`→`dlloverride`, `filestocopy`+`prermfakedll`→`file` (+ fake-DLL removal), `postinstallreboot`→`wineserver -k` + wineboot, `useif platform|locale`→recipe `when` clause, `cxdiagcheck`→preflight. Medals map to a `status`/`reports` field.
* Keep the per-app `LC_ALL` pattern (registry `HKCU\Software\Wine\LC_ALL` under a locale condition) rather than a global `LC_ALL`.
* `WINED3DMETAL=1` in 91 ties means a CX-bottle importer must treat `WINED3DMETAL`/`WINEDXVK`/`D3DMetal` as aliases of `renderer`.
